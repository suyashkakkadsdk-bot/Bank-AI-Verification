BANK ACCOUNT SERVICES

Savings Account

A savings account is used for regular personal banking needs such as depositing money, withdrawing money, transferring funds, and receiving payments.

Account Opening

To open a savings account, a customer must provide the required identity and address documents according to the bank's applicable account-opening requirements.

Account Closure

A customer can request closure of a savings account by following the bank's account closure procedure. Any applicable outstanding charges or requirements must be completed before closure.

Balance Inquiry

Customers can check their account balance through supported banking channels such as internet banking, mobile banking, ATM services, or branch services.

Fund Transfer

Customers can transfer funds through supported banking channels. Transactions may be subject to applicable limits, verification requirements, and bank rules.
